# Operations & Workforce Capacity Planning Model

## Introduction
Welcome to the **Operations Planning Analytics** repository! This project presents an operational capacity, labor utilization, and budget control model designed to evaluate workforce allocation across multiple global manufacturing and operational divisions (Assembly, Packaging, and Management). By analyzing planned vs. actual labor hours, overtime trends, and budget variances, this model enables workforce optimization and operational cost control.

## The Problem
Operations and plant managers frequently encounter resource allocation inefficiencies, unexpected labor budget overruns, and capacity imbalances across production locations. Key challenges include:
* **Overtime & Unworked Hour Fluctuations:** Difficulty tracking non-productive (unworked) hours alongside excessive overtime expenditures.
* **Budget vs. Earned Cost Variances:** Lack of real-time visibility into whether labor allocations align with projected financial budgets across global hubs (Toronto, Tokyo, Chicago, Mumbai, etc.).
* **Workforce & Job Title Mismatches:** Inability to balance resource workloads between Assemblers, Packaging Operators, and Coordinators across operational divisions.


## The Solution
This project provides a comprehensive, pivot-analyzed workforce planning and operational control model that turns raw labor logs into key management insights:
* **Division-Wise Work Distribution:** Tracks planned hours vs. actual worked hours across Assembly, Packaging, and Management divisions to identify bottleneck areas.
* **Budget & Financial Variance Tracking:** Compares allocated labor budgets ($28.78M) against total amount earned ($30.13M) to pinpoint cost drivers.
* **Overtime & Capacity Analysis:** Analyzes top individual overtime drivers ($2.75M total overtime cost) and city-level overtime patterns across 250+ employee operations.


## Tools Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data structuring, advanced formulas, multi-dimensional pivot tables, and analytical summary layout.
* **Power Query / Data Modeling:** For data cleaning, field transformations, and calculating key metrics (Unworked Amount, Overtime Earnings, Variance).
